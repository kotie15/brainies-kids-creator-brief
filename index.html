Can't Focus On Schoolwork

Smart, just not on homework

Brainies is calm focus for the kid who locks in on what he loves but falls apart at homework.

Who this isYour kid is smart, has potential and everyone knows it. He'll build Lego or play a game for hours without looking up. Then homework comes out and five minutes in he's staring at the ceiling, distracting himself, trying everything in their power to avoid doing something what he/she doesn't like. They forget it, throw tantrums and ultimately never hand it in. Every teacher says the same thing: so much potential, if he'd just apply himself.
The transformationFrom a two-hour homework fight to sitting down and getting it done. The teacher's praise of him actually focusing is good news.
Simple outline — say it your way
  • Open (0-5s)Say your hook. The contrast: hours on what he loves, spends minutes on homework/disinterested in homework.
  • BodyWhat homework nights looked like, what the teachers kept saying, what you tried. Then Brainies.
  • Kids are still learning self regulation, this simply helps support them, giving them calm focus.
  • CloseHomework done without the fight. Him proud of himself.
Talking points
  • Homework is a war every single night.
  • He reads the same sentence three times. Stares at the ceiling.Seems completely spaced out, doodles on his paper.
  • So much potential if they’d just apply themselves and open up to trying instead of distracting themselves every second with screens and toys.
  • Told brainies that supported his calm focus state so that he is not distracted.
Hooks

🌱 The StoryElie, Dr. Paria, and why saffron at the clinical dose
03 · The story

Built around one hero ingredient

Elie founded Brainies and partnered with Dr. Paria, a naturopathic doctor who specializes in hormones and stress. Together they built the formula around our hero ingredient: saffron.

Why saffron? Saffron is the most expensive spice in the world, and for good reason.

  • The extract inside it is one of the most studied natural mood ingredients on the planet.
  • In clinical trials, saffron extract has been shown to lift mood and calm the stress response at the same time.

Here's the problem: in commercial use saffron almost always gets sold in pill form, and usually at doses too small to matter.

Brainies was one of the first to put saffron into a gummy at the full clinical dose, 28mg of patented Affron extract per day. The same amount used in the actual studies. Not a sprinkle for the label.

Then they gave it supporting:

L-TheanineThe calm-focus ingredient from green tea.
GABAYour brain's natural "off switch" for racing thoughts.
PhosphatidylserineProtects your brain from long-term stress wear.

Saffron on its own is powerful. All four together is what makes Brainies a super gummy.

🎥 How to Film ItFour rules that separate good videos from great ones
05 · How to film it

Four rules

1
Start with your hook the second you press record.

No intro, no "hey guys." Say the hook word for word, then it's all yours.

2
Front camera, natural light, no setup.

Kitchen, car, bedroom, wherever you actually are. It should look like your normal content, not an ad.

3
Talk like you're telling a friend about your kid.

Your own words, your own pace. Don't read anything. If you stumble, keep going, that's the real part.

4
Show the gummies at some point.

Hold the pouch up, whatever's natural. Doesn't need to be the first shot.

Bonus, not a rule: keep it under 60 seconds. If it runs long, that's fine, send it anyway.
